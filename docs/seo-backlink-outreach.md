# Ritera backlink outreach campaign

This campaign prioritises editorially relevant links to pages that help authors make a publishing decision. It avoids bulk directory submissions, paid link networks, and repeated exact-match anchor text.

## Pages to promote

| Priority | Page | Best reason to link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `https://riterapublishing.com/` | Evidence-led overview of Ritera's services, catalogue, authors, and case studies |
| 2 | `https://riterapublishing.com/case-studies` | Documented publishing workflows and author outcomes |
| 3 | `https://riterapublishing.com/blog/how-to-publish-a-book-in-india-step-by-step-guide-2026` | Practical Indian publishing process guide |
| 4 | `https://riterapublishing.com/blog/how-much-does-self-publishing-cost-in-india-complete-guide-for-first-time-authors` | Transparent self-publishing cost guide |
| 5 | `https://riterapublishing.com/packages` | Commercial page for readers already comparing publishing services |
| 6 | Individual author and book pages | Strong destination for interviews, reviews, and author-profile coverage |

Suggested distribution: homepage 30%, case studies 20%, guides 25%, packages 10%, author/book pages 15%. Use natural anchors such as the brand name, article title, author name, or book title.

## Owned-profile links to verify

Update Ritera's Google Business Profile, LinkedIn company page, Medium profile, YouTube About section, Instagram bio, and any event-speaker profiles so each uses the canonical non-`www` homepage. Where a platform supports additional links, use the packages or case-studies page only when it matches the profile content.

## Outreach drafts

### Author interview or book feature

Subject: Interview idea: [Author] on [specific expertise] and publishing [Book]

Hello [Name],

I am reaching out from Ritera Publishing with a possible interview for your readers. [Author] is a [one-sentence verified credential] and the author of *[Book]*. A useful angle would be [specific lesson relevant to the publication's audience], with practical examples from the book and the author's experience.

Author background: [author-page URL]

Book details: [book-page URL]

We can provide a review copy, original answers, and approved images. If the story is a fit, please cite the author or book page so readers can verify the details.

### Expert contribution

Subject: Sourced article proposal: [working title]

Hello [Name],

I would like to propose an original article for [Publication]: “[Title].” It would explain [reader problem] using Government of India ISBN and copyright references, real publishing examples, and a clear checklist. It would be written for your audience and would not duplicate an article on Ritera's site.

Suggested outline:

1. [Section]
2. [Section]
3. [Section]

Relevant background and source material: [guide or case-study URL]

We will follow your editorial and disclosure requirements. If accepted, we would appreciate a natural source or author-bio link where your policy permits it.

## Tracking

Record the target, contact date, page pitched, response, publication URL, link destination, anchor text, and whether the link is followed. Review earned links monthly in Search Console and remove any target that asks for hidden, sitewide, or unrelated paid links.
